# Marketing

## Brand is Contamos; copy says "group" and "ledger", never "household"
The product is named Contamos (renamed from Hormiga on 2026-09-07). It is positioned as a shared multi-currency ledger for any group that shares money (couples, flatmates, families, side projects), not for households specifically and not as accounting software. Marketing copy must not say "household", "hogar" or "casa"; use "group", "everyone you share money with" and "ledger" ("libro" in Spanish). Repo, directory and package slugs still read "hormiga" on purpose.
