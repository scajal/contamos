# Images

## Contamos wordmark is always lowercase; BrandMark is the isotype

The logo text reads "contamos" in lowercase wherever it appears as a wordmark (header and footer lockups, OG image). In prose, legal lines and SEO names the product is still "Contamos". The isotype is the BrandMark component (resources/js/components/brand-mark.tsx): a rounded plate in foreground colour, three ledger lines in background colour, and a lime #b2e016 dot; it inverts itself on dark surfaces, so never add a PNG variant for dark mode. favicon.svg, favicon.ico, apple-touch-icon.png, images/iso-logo.png and images/og.png are rasterised from the same 64-grid geometry.
